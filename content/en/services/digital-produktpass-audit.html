The Digital Product Passport is more than a compliance requirement

With the introduction of the EU Digital Product Passport (DPP), requirements for product transparency, sustainability, and product information are increasing. For many organizations, it is becoming clear that the real challenge is not only regulatory compliance but also the quality and governance of their product data.

Incomplete product information, missing sustainability data, fragmented supplier information, and insufficient product data governance not only make DPP compliance more difficult but also limit initiatives such as AI Search, Agentic Commerce, and digital product experiences.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Digital Product Passport Audit?

The audit evaluates how well your organization is prepared for upcoming Digital Product Passport requirements. It assesses your product data landscape, data quality, governance, processes, and systems. You receive a readiness score, a gap analysis, and actionable recommendations for moving forward.

Why should we start preparing for the Digital Product Passport now?

Although DPP requirements will be introduced gradually, building the necessary product data foundation, governance structures, and processes takes time. Starting early reduces risk and prepares your business for future AI and commerce initiatives.

Is the Digital Product Passport only about compliance?

No. The Digital Product Passport often reveals underlying challenges in product data quality, governance, and processes. Addressing these issues creates long-term business value beyond regulatory compliance.

Is our existing PIM system sufficient?

In many cases, yes. The biggest challenges are typically data quality, governance, process maturity, and integration across systems rather than the technology itself. The audit identifies where improvements are needed.

Which systems are included in the assessment?

Depending on your environment, we assess PIM, ERP, DAM, commerce platforms, supplier data sources, and the data flows between these systems.

What deliverables will we receive?

You receive a clear assessment of your DPP readiness together with a practical action plan covering the organizational, process, and technical capabilities required for the Digital Product Passport.

Who is this audit designed for?

The audit is intended for organizations with large product assortments, multiple product data sources, supplier-driven product information, and increasing requirements around sustainability, compliance, and product data governance.

How does the audit work?

The audit starts with an assessment of your current product data landscape. We then evaluate your DPP readiness, identify gaps across data, processes, and governance, and develop a prioritized roadmap for implementation.

What are the benefits of a system-agnostic approach?

Our audit is independent of any software vendor. This allows us to objectively assess your existing landscape and recommend the most appropriate improvements based on your business requirements.

What are the possible next steps after the audit?

The audit often serves as the starting point for broader initiatives such as Product Data Governance programs, PIM optimization or migration, supplier data integration, product data quality initiatives, AI-powered Product Data Management, and Agentic Commerce enablement.
