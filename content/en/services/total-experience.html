What does Total Experience mean?

Total Experience refers to a holistic ecosystem of skills, services, and experts in digital marketing and consulting. Maximum impact is based on the interaction of the individual areas—tailored to your brand, your goals, and your target groups.

 
Originally conceived by the Gartner Group as a strategy to intelligently link customer experience (CX), employee experience (EX), and user experience (UX), we now take Total Experience a step further—as a real growth driver for modern brands.
