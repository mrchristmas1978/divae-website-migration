Why PIM?

Product Data Makes the Difference

Growing product ranges, more demanding customers and more and more sales channels are making product data management increasingly complex. The right product information management (PIM) system provides targeted relief here.

The right system but also the right processes.

The right system is often the deciding factor. After all, not all PIMs are the same - the market is littered with countless solutions and providers. However, the system should be accepted by the employees, fit in with the internal processes and cover the individual requirements as far as possible.

Faster Time-to-Market

With a PIM system, new products or updates can be rolled out to online stores, catalogs or marketplaces much faster - and in a channel-specific and automated manner.

Scalability & Internationalization

Languages, markets, requirements: A PIM system makes it possible to prepare your product data effortlessly for different target groups and countries.
