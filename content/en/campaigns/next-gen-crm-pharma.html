Why now is the right time to rethink your CRM strategy

The upcoming Veeva–Salesforce split has major implications for the CRM strategies of many pharma companies: Starting in 2025, Veeva will no longer offer its CRM products on the Salesforce platform. Pharma and MedTech organizations must now decide whether to migrate to the new Veeva Vault CRM, switch to the new Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud launching in September — or take this opportunity to develop an entirely new CRM strategy.

Our Framework:*

Discovery & Vision Alignment

In a tailored executive workshop, we define your strategic goals, prioritize relevant use cases, and co-create a clear North Star vision with measurable success metrics.

Data Health Check

We assess your current data model, CRM customizations (e.g. Veeva Vault or IQVIA), and system integrations to identify migration blockers, readiness gaps, and critical areas that require adjustments for a smooth transition.

CRM Strategy & Roadmap

Based on this analysis, we develop a long-term CRM vision and a detailed rollout plan — including phases, milestones, and risk management — to ensure your transformation remains scalable and controllable.

Change Readiness & Adoption Plan

We design role-based training paths, support the development of super-user communities, and create communication toolkits to actively engage your teams in the change process. This builds acceptance, minimizes resistance, and ensures the long-term success of your platform.

This lays the foundation for a scalable, future-ready CRM architecture — one that connects processes, breaks down silos, and delivers real business value.
