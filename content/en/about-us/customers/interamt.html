With around 90,000 job postings published annually, INTERAMT is Germany’s largest job portal exclusively dedicated to the public sector. As a leading provider of e-recruiting solutions in the public sector, the platform has connected job seekers and employers from federal, state, and local authorities on a central platform since 2008.

INTERAMT is operated by DVZ Mecklenburg-Vorpommern GmbH, which manages the job portal as an independent subsidiary.

The initial situation

INTERAMT as a Digital Job Placement Platform

The INTERAMT project was launched in 2008 in collaboration with Deutsche Telekom and later continued by DVZ Mecklenburg-Vorpommern GmbH. The original goal was to develop a platform to place Telekom civil servants into new areas of employment.

 
Over the years, INTERAMT evolved into the largest online job portal for the public sector in Germany.

 
To keep the platform technologically up to date, build a modern software architecture, and ensure long-term maintenance and further development, diva-e Conclusion was brought on board as a technology partner.

The goal

A Modern Digital Job Portal for the Public Sector

The goal of the project was to build a modern digital job portal with an intuitive application management system tailored specifically to the requirements of the public sector.

 
The focus was on efficiency, data security, and usability with the ambition to optimize the digital experience for both public authorities and applicants while sustainably simplifying application processes.

The implementation

End-to-End Project Support from Analysis to Continuous Development

Over 30 developers from diva-e Conclusion supported the project over many years across various phases – from requirements analysis and implementation to quality assurance and documentation.

The result

Millions of Job Searches and Awarded Top Career Portal 2025

Today, INTERAMT is the leading career portal for the public sector in Germany—with impressive key figures:

  • Over 245,000 active applicants
  • More than 4,500 public authorities as partners
  • Around 75,000 job postings published annually
  • Over 1 million applications per year
  • More than 25 million job searches since project launch
  • 45,000 applicant profiles
  • Top Career Portal 2025 (Focus Business)

 
The success of INTERAMT is reflected in continuously growing user numbers, high customer satisfaction with a recommendation rate of over 90%, and innovative features such as the integration of anonymous application processes.

 
In addition, the platform fully complies with accessibility requirements in accordance with EN 301 549 V.2.1.2 and WCAG 2.1, Level AA.

 
We are proud to continue supporting INTERAMT as a digital experience partner and to further develop the platform with new features on an ongoing basis.
