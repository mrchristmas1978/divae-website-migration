Important decision: Veeva or Salesforce?

The technical partnership between Salesforce and Veeva will end in 2025. In future, Veeva will run on its own platform. This means: From September 1 in 2025, Veeva will no longer develop new functions for the CRM on the Salesforce platform. For many companies in the pharmaceutical industry, this means rethinking their own CRM strategy. Our experts at diva-e Conclusion will help you find the right path - secure, scalable and integrated into your digital infrastructure.

 
As a Salesforce Summit Partner with a proven pharmaceutical focus, we offer a customized “Move from Veeva to Salesforce” audit, which consists of the following steps:

  • Analysis of your current Veeva landscape (data, processes, integrations)
  • Fit-gap workshop on the life sciences cloud roadmap
  • Migration blueprint including effort estimation, risk evaluation and migration plan
