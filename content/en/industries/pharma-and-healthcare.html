Mastering challenges with diva-e Conclusion

Digitization in the healthcare sector in Germany has increased steadily in recent years. Among other things, the COVID 19 pandemic led to a significant, albeit unavoidable, push toward digital offerings. The pharmaceutical industry and the healthcare sector are currently facing the following, major challenges:

 
Innovative competition

 
Traditional pharmaceutical companies are increasingly competing with technology companies and startups offering innovative digital solutions. We help you adapt to these new market dynamics and rethink your current processes.

 
Integration of digital technologies

 
From the analysis of relevant customer and product data to automated processes: Pharma 4.0 describes the increasing networking of devices, systems and people involved in pharmaceutical or biotechnological production. The implementation of artificial intelligence (AI) and Internet of Things (IoT) requires a digital strategy. We develop solutions for Pharma & Healthcare under regulatory requirements and with business added value.

 
Strategies for Big Data

 
The increasing availability of big data from different sources such as clinical trials, patient records and social media requires efficient data management strategies. We support you in processing your data to improve product development and communication with internal and external target groups.

 
Complex data protection

 
With growing networking and increased data exchange, the risk of cyberattacks is also increasing. Therefore, it is important that the healthcare industry has secure IT systems in place to prevent data breaches. Data protection regulations such as the DSGVO must be ensured at all times.

 
Personalized communication and services

 
In the course of digitalization, personalized services are becoming increasingly important. We develop tailored omnichannel marketing concepts for the pharmaceutical and healthcare industries to better address their target groups and position products optimally.
