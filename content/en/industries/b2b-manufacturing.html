Complex and Heterogeneous System Landscapes

In B2B, system landscapes often evolve historically - departmentalized systems or an insufficient IT infrastructure can lead to challenges in data exchange. Resolving these issues often requires a tailored solution.

We support you in breaking down silos and building your system landscape in a way that ensures smooth data flow between different ERP, PIM, and CRM systems and other interfaces, thereby laying the foundation for an exceptional customer experience.

Data Protection and IT Security

The intelligent networking of all business areas increases the risk of cybercrime. B2B companies often work with external suppliers and business partners who are granted selective access to the IT infrastructure - this poses risks such as data breaches or data leaks.

With our tailored cloud solutions, you can optimize your IT infrastructure while ensuring the security of your data.
